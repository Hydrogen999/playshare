Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use PlaySphere, we collect:

  • Account info: email, username, password (encrypted).
  • Profile info: avatar, bio, country, level, XP, PlayCoin balance.
  • Gameplay: matches played, wins/losses, wagers, tournament entries.
  • Payments: transaction records (payment details are handled by our payment processor, Paystack).
  • Technical: IP address, browser type, device info, error logs.

2. How We Use Your Info

  • Provide and improve the Platform.
  • Process payments and deliver virtual goods.
  • Moderate content and prevent fraud/abuse.
  • Send important account notifications.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing Your Info

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Payment processors (Paystack) — to process your purchases.
  • Hosting providers (Render, Neon) — to run the Platform.
  • Law enforcement — only when legally required.

4. Public Information

Your username, avatar, level, bio, and country are visible to other players. Do not include private information in your bio.

5. Security

We use industry-standard security (HTTPS, encrypted passwords, secure tokens). However, no system is 100% secure. Use a strong, unique password.

6. Cookies & Storage

We use browser localStorage for your login session. We do not use third-party tracking cookies at this time.

7. Your Rights

You can request to view, correct, or delete your data at any time by contacting us. Account deletion is available on request.

8. Children

PlaySphere is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. Significant changes will be communicated through the Platform.

10. Contact

Email: privacy@playsphere.com
