Refund Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Digital Goods Policy

PlaySphere sells virtual goods — PlayCoins, Battle Pass access, Premium subscriptions, and cosmetic items. Because these are digital products delivered instantly, all sales are final and generally non-refundable.

2. When Refunds May Apply

We may issue refunds in the following cases:

  • Duplicate charge — you were charged twice for the same purchase.
  • Purchase failed to deliver — you paid but did not receive PlayCoins or Premium access.
  • Unauthorized transaction — someone used your payment method without permission (verification required).
  • Platform error — a bug in PlaySphere caused the wrong charge.

3. When Refunds Do Not Apply

  • You changed your mind after purchasing.
  • You spent the PlayCoins before requesting a refund.
  • Your account was banned for violating the Terms of Service.
  • You forgot to cancel a Premium subscription.
  • You lost a match and regret a wager.

4. How to Request a Refund

Email us at support@playsphere.com with:

  • Your PlaySphere username.
  • The payment reference (from your Paystack receipt).
  • The date and amount of the transaction.
  • The reason for the refund request.

5. Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days. Refunds are returned via your original payment method through Paystack. Timing of the actual return depends on your bank or card issuer.

6. Premium Subscriptions

Premium subscriptions do not auto-renew. When your subscription ends, no further charges occur unless you manually purchase again. Partial refunds for unused Premium time are not offered.

7. Chargebacks

Filing a chargeback without first contacting us will result in immediate account suspension while we investigate. Please always contact us first — we resolve most issues within 48 hours.

8. Contact

Email: support@playsphere.com
